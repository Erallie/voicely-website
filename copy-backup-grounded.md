## Voicely Text

**Tagline:** Give every message a voice.

**Description:** Read messages from a voice channel's text chat aloud in that same voice channel, with per-user accent, region, and nickname settings.

**What it does:** Messages from voice-channel chat, spoken aloud.

1. **Voice-channel text-to-speech:** Read messages from a voice channel's associated text chat aloud in that same channel.
2. **Automatic or on-demand speech:** Enable autoread when you join voice, speak one message on demand, or skip messages you do not want played.
3. **Accent and region settings:** Save your preferred accent and region, with optional overrides for a single message.
4. **Custom name pronunciation:** Set a nickname globally or for one server so names and mentions are read the way you expect.

## Voicely Ping

**Tagline:** Know when the conversation starts.

**Description:** Watch selected voice channels and receive a direct message when one reaches your chosen number of participants.

**What it does:** Personal alerts for voice-channel activity.

1. **Direct-message notifications:** Choose a voice channel and the participant count that should trigger a private alert.
2. **Multiple channels and thresholds:** Create alerts for several channels or multiple participant counts in the same channel.
3. **Recurring silent hours:** Choose the days, times, and time zone when notifications should be withheld.
4. **Do Not Disturb:** Pause all notifications temporarily and resume them when you are ready.

## Voicely Role

**Tagline:** Notify your server when voice chat becomes active.

**Description:** Post a role or @everyone notification when a selected voice channel reaches a participant threshold, then update it as people join and leave.

**What it does:** Server-wide notifications for active voice channels.

1. **Role or @everyone notifications:** Choose who to notify and how many counted participants should trigger the message.
2. **Multiple channels and thresholds:** Watch one or many voice channels and configure independent notifications for each.
3. **Live message updates:** Update the participant count as people join and leave, then mark the gathering as ended.
4. **Custom messages and destinations:** Post in a text channel or voice-channel side chat, with custom text for active and ended notifications.

## Voicely Translate

**Tagline:** Help every voice be understood.

**Description:** Transcribe each speaker and post the original speech with translations in the voice channel's side chat.

**What it does:** Live transcription and translation in voice-channel side chat.

1. **Supports all BCP 47 language codes:** Translate messages in any language, including dialects and regional variations.
2. **Translate multiple languages at once:** Choose which languages to translate messages into, and let Voicely Translate do the rest.
3. **Voicely Text integration:** Read translated messages aloud in the voice channel with Voicely Text.
4. **An affordable solution:** Only pay for the tokens you use, with no monthly subscription required.
