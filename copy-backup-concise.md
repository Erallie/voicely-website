## Voicely Text

**Tagline:** Give every message a voice.

**Description:** Have messages from voice-channel chat spoken aloud with your preferred accent, region, and nickname.

**What it does:** Take part in voice chat without having to speak.

1. **Join the conversation through text:** Send a message in voice-channel chat and have it spoken aloud to everyone in the call.
2. **Speak automatically or on demand:** Use autoread, speak one message on demand, or skip anything you do not want played.
3. **Make every message sound more like you:** Save an accent and region, or override them for one message.
4. **Make names easier to understand:** Replace long, stylized, or mispronounced display names with a global or server nickname.

## Voicely Ping

**Tagline:** Know when the conversation starts.

**Description:** Choose channels and participant thresholds, then get a private message when enough people gather.

**What it does:** Stop checking channels. Let the conversation come to you.

1. **Know when people have gathered:** Get a private message when a channel reaches your participant threshold.
2. **Follow every channel you care about:** Watch several channels or set multiple thresholds for one channel.
3. **Keep notifications on your schedule:** Set silent hours by day, time, and time zone—even for overnight periods.
4. **Pause everything with Do Not Disturb:** Pause every notification until you turn Do Not Disturb off.

## Voicely Role

**Tagline:** Help active voice chats grow.

**Description:** Invite more people into active voice chat with threshold-based role notifications that update as the channel changes.

**What it does:** Give active voice chats the chance to grow.

1. **Notify the people most likely to join:** Ping a role or @everyone when a channel reaches your chosen participant count.
2. **Support every kind of gathering:** Watch multiple channels with independent notifications and thresholds.
3. **Keep the invitation up to date:** Show the live participant count, then update the message when everyone leaves.
4. **Make notifications fit your server:** Choose a text channel or voice side chat and customize active and ending messages.

## Voicely Translate

**Tagline:** Help every voice be understood.

**Description:** Let everyone speak their own language while following one conversation through live transcripts and translations.

**What it does:** Cost-friendly translation for multilingual voice conversations.

1. **Supports all BCP 47 language codes:** Translate messages in any language, including dialects and regional variations.
2. **Translate between more than two languages:** Use three or more languages in one session so everyone can follow in a language they understand.
3. **Voicely Text integration:** Read translated messages aloud in the voice channel with Voicely Text.
4. **An affordable solution:** Pay only for your server's transcription and translation usage—no monthly subscription required.
